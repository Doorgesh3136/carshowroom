        Sell or tradeyour car.

        A fair price based on today's market, a firm offer the same day, and no commission. We buy outright or take it against any car in our stock.

        Three steps, usually one visit

        1. Send the detailsMake, model, year, mileage and a phone number. Photos help if you have them.
        2. Get a guide priceWe reply during sales hours with a price range based on recent sales in Mauritius.
        3. Bring it inA 20-minute check at the showroom, then a firm offer. Paid by bank transfer, or knocked off your next car.

        Questions people ask

        Do you buy cars with finance still owing?

        Yes. We settle the balance with your bank directly and pay you the difference.

        Which cars do you buy?

        Most makes, normally under 10 years old. We also take older cars as a trade-in against one of ours.

        How long does it take to get paid?

        Usually the same day the paperwork is signed, by bank transfer.

        Is there a fee?

        No. The valuation and the check are free, and there is no commission.

        Home/Privacy policy

        Privacy policy

        Last updated 31 October 2026

        Your Dream Motors ("we", "us") runs this website. This page explains what personal data we collect, why, and your rights under the Data Protection Act 2017 of Mauritius.

        What we collect

        When you send a form, it opens WhatsApp with the details you typed: your name, phone number and message, and for valuations the details of your car. We receive that message on WhatsApp. We do not store form entries on this website.

        Your saved cars and your cookie choice are kept in your own browser (local storage). They never leave your device.

        If you accept analytics cookies, we collect anonymous usage data (pages viewed, device type, approximate country) to improve the site. If you choose "Essential only", no analytics run.

        Why we use it

        To answer your enquiry, arrange test drives and valuations, and complete a sale if you buy or sell a car with us. We do not sell your data or use it for unrelated marketing.

        How long we keep it

        Enquiries are kept for up to 24 months. Sales records are kept for as long as Mauritian law requires.

        Who we share it with

        Only with those needed to complete your purchase, such as the National Transport Authority for registration, and your bank or insurer if you ask us to. WhatsApp (Meta) carries messages you send us.

        Your rights

        You can ask to see, correct or delete the data we hold about you, or object to how we use it. Email info@yourdreammotors.mu or call +230 433 8026. You can also complain to the Data Protection Office of Mauritius.
